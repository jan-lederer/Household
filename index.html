Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

About this policy

This policy covers the Dwell app for Android, iOS, and the web, as well as this page. Dwell is maintained by Jan Lederer.

Information stored on your device

Dwell can store your selected server address, sign-in token, selected household, theme preference, and (when you choose offline mode) your personal household and chores on your device. On supported platforms, sign-in credentials are stored using the platform's secure storage. Offline household data is stored in the app's private storage. Demo content is sample data.

Signing out removes your saved sign-in token and selected-household choice. Other locally saved information, including offline household data and preferences, remains until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Android or iCloud device backup behavior may also depend on your device settings.

Information sent to a Dwell server

If you connect to a server and create or join an account, Dwell sends the name and email address you provide, your password for account authentication, and the household and chore information you enter. The server stores passwords as salted hashes and issues a sign-in token; Dwell stores that token securely on your device and sends it with authenticated requests.

The server you choose stores account, household, membership, and chore records in its database. The operator of that server can access and manage this information. You choose the server address in Dwell; the app does not send this information to a separate Dwell analytics or advertising service.

Use a server configured for HTTPS to protect information while it travels over the network. A server using plain HTTP does not encrypt that traffic.

Analytics, advertising, and sharing

Dwell does not include advertising or analytics services. Household information is shared with the members of that household through its server. The app does not sell personal information.

Network and page hosting

The app contacts only the Dwell server address you configure. This privacy policy is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor information, including IP addresses, for security and service operation. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Your choices and data requests

You can sign out or clear local app data to remove locally stored information. To request access to or deletion of information held by a Dwell server, contact that server's operator. If you use a server maintained by the app publisher, contact the publisher through the Dwell project page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app publisher through the Dwell project page.
